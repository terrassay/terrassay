# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Terrassay to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish others' personal information.
- Follow the law and the project's security and data policies.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, scraping, or manipulative behaviour.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events, and private communication about the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact in the organisation profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants, depending on severity. A maintainer involved in an incident recuses themselves.
